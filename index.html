What FormEasy does

Give it your class roster and a grades CSV, and it lays them straight onto the official DepEd report-card PDF — correct fonts, lines and layout, nothing redrawn by hand. SF9 is ready now, with SF10 and more forms on the way. By default, everything stays in this browser. Cloud upload only happens when you sign in and explicitly save a form set.
